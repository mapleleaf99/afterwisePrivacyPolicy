引言

欢迎使用「日后」（Afterwise）。我们重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理与隐私相关的信息。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

  • 你创建的内容：决策、选项、理由、复盘结果、标签等，保存在你的设备本地，并可通过 Apple iCloud 在你自己的设备之间同步。
  • 我们不运营自建业务服务器来存储你的日记内容，因此开发者无法像传统账号系统那样在自有后台查看你的决策正文。
  • 订阅与支付：由 Apple App Store / StoreKit 处理。我们无法获取你的完整银行卡等支付敏感信息。
  • 诊断信息（如有）：若你通过系统向 Apple 或开发者反馈崩溃等问题，可能包含设备型号、系统版本等技术信息，仅用于排查问题。

2. 信息如何使用

上述信息仅用于提供产品功能，包括：记录与复盘、本地提醒、跨设备同步、导出、小组件与系统搜索等。我们不会出售你的个人内容。

3. 权限说明

  • 通知：用于在约定的复盘日提醒你回看决定。你可以随时在系统设置中关闭。

4. 第三方服务

本应用依赖 Apple 提供的能力与服务，例如：App Store、StoreKit、iCloud / CloudKit、系统通知、Spotlight、小组件与快捷指令等。这些服务受 Apple 相关政策约束。

5. 数据存储、同步与删除

  • 内容默认存储在你的设备；开启 iCloud 后，同步至你的 Apple 账户对应的 iCloud 私有数据。
  • 你可以在 App 内删除记录；也可通过删除 App、管理 iCloud 存储等方式移除相关数据。
  • 取消订阅请在 Apple ID 的订阅管理中操作。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策。更新后会在本页面调整「更新日期」。继续使用即表示你知悉更新后的内容。

Introduction

Welcome to Afterwise (“日后”). We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles privacy-related information.

1. Information We Handle

  • Content you create: decisions, options, reasons, review results, tags, and similar data are stored on your device and may sync across your devices via Apple iCloud.
  • No self-operated content server: we do not run our own backend to store your journal content, so we cannot browse your decision text on a private server like a traditional account system.
  • Subscriptions & payments: processed by the Apple App Store / StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Diagnostics (if any): crash or feedback reports may include technical details such as device model and OS version, used only for troubleshooting.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to provide product features such as logging and review, local reminders, cross-device sync, export, widgets, and system search. We do not sell your personal content.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: used to remind you to review decisions on scheduled dates. You can turn this off in system settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

The app relies on Apple services and frameworks, including the App Store, StoreKit, iCloud / CloudKit, system notifications, Spotlight, widgets, and App Shortcuts. Those services are governed by Apple’s policies.

5. Storage, Sync, and Deletion

  • Content is stored on your device by default; with iCloud enabled, it syncs to private iCloud data associated with your Apple ID.
  • You may delete records in the app, or remove related data by deleting the app / managing iCloud storage.
  • To cancel a subscription, manage it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date on this page will be revised accordingly.
